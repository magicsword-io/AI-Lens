Download the ZIP for **Windows x64**, **Linux x64**, or **macOS Apple Silicon**
below. Extract it, keep the executable beside its engine library, and follow
the [getting started guide](https://magicsword-io.github.io/AI-Lens/getting-started/).
No Rust toolchain is needed for downloaded packages.

## What's included

- Seven native hook adapters: Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, Gemini CLI, Cursor,
  Copilot CLI and Cowork. The last three remain experimental.
- Eleven observation-only telemetry sources, including OTLP and provider audit
  imports. Enterprise audit feeds require separate administrator access.
- Thirty example YAML rules, synthetic demo, configuration examples and docs.
- MagicSword branding and Apache-2.0 licensing.

The native build, protocol, rule, telemetry and extracted-package checks run
on all three release platforms before publication. `SHA256SUMS` and
`build-provenance.json` identify the exact ZIPs, source, toolchain and builders.
Earlier real Claude/Codex acceptance and remaining gaps are documented in
the [verification record](https://github.com/magicsword-io/AI-Lens/blob/main/docs/verification.md).

## Before running

Windows needs the [Microsoft Visual C++ v14 x64 Redistributable](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist#latest-supported-redistributable-version).
Linux/macOS extraction may require `chmod +x ./agent-lens`. Packages are unsigned;
macOS may require approving the downloaded app in Privacy & Security after review.
Intel Macs and other architectures can [build from source](https://github.com/magicsword-io/AI-Lens#build-from-source).

Cursor/Cowork full installed-client acceptance, Gemini whole-CLI trust/provider
delivery, broader Copilot platform/event behavior and enterprise feeds remain
unverified. The Ubuntu Codex live lane is blocked by an AppArmor prerequisite.
Hooks depend on the client invoking and honoring them; this is not an OS sandbox.
